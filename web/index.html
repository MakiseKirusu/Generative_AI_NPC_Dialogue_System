Maple Hollow

A village at the edge of the Whispering Woods. Since the Old Mine collapsed, the miners are gone and their record book with them; and now Elder Mira has lost her silver locket among the wolves. You are a traveller who just walked in through the North Gate.

Talk to the villagers by typing anything you like. They answer with an AI, but the game decides what really happens: what you can buy, which tasks you get and what they pay.
